## IAQ is more than a filter meme

Wildfire smoke season, allergies, and sealed modern homes mean indoor air deserves a plan — but not every product on a tech’s checklist belongs in your house.

## Practical starting points

1. Right-sized filtration your system can handle
2. Consistent maintenance
3. Addressing humidity and ventilation where needed
4. Targeted purification only when it solves a real problem

## Smoke season tips

- Know your filter status before the sky turns orange
- Limit outdoor air intake during peak smoke
- Ask about filtration upgrades that won’t choke your blower
