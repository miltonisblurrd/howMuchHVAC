## Pre-summer (do this before July)

- Replace or check filters
- Clear debris around the outdoor unit
- Test cooling on a warm afternoon
- Note any odd smells, noises, or weak airflow
- Book a pro tune-up if it?s been 12+ months

## Pre-winter

- Test heat before the first cold night
- Check carbon monoxide detectors (for furnace homes)
- Listen for hard starts or delayed ignition
- Schedule safety-focused heating service if anything feels off

## What the pro visit should include

Electrical checks, airflow, drainage, refrigerant performance indicators, safety observations, and a clear written summary ? not a 10-minute filter slap.
