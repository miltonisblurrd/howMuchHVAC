## Start with a real diagnostic

If a company leads with “you need a new system” before testing, pause. A legitimate diagnostic looks at performance, safety, age, refrigerant type, and repair history.

## Repair often wins when…

- The issue is isolated (capacitor, contactor, sensor, clogged drain)
- The system is relatively young
- Efficiency is still acceptable for your bills and comfort
- You’ve had few prior major repairs

## Replacement makes sense when…

- Repairs are stacking year after year
- Major components are failing together
- Comfort problems are systemic (wrong size, failing heat exchanger)
- Obsolete constraints make parts scarce or inefficient
