## The short answer

AC replacement in Orange County typically varies based on system size, efficiency, duct condition, electrical needs, and install complexity — not a single Facebook-ad number.

## What actually changes the price

- **Tonnage / capacity** matched to your home (not guesswork)
- **Efficiency rating** and brand tier
- **Ductwork condition** — leaks can force extra work
- **Access and labor** (tight side yards, second-story condensers)
- **Permits and code requirements**

## Good / Better / Best — without the games

When replacement is truly needed, we present options with clear tradeoffs: upfront cost, efficiency, noise, warranty, and comfort features. You’re never forced into the top tier.

## Red flags in other quotes

- No diagnostic — just a replacement pitch
- “Today only” pricing
- Vague scope (“complete system”) with no equipment details
- Ignoring ducts when rooms are uneven
