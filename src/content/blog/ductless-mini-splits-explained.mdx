## Perfect use cases

- ADUs and guest suites
- Garages converted to living space
- Home offices that never cool down
- Additions where extending ducts would be invasive

## How a mini-split works (plain English)

An outdoor unit connects to one or more indoor heads. Each head can usually set its own temperature ? true zoning without rebuilding your whole duct system.

## Install expectations

Line-set routing, electrical, condensate, and mounting. A clean install looks intentional. A bad one looks like an afterthought. We obsess over the first.
