## The expensive mistake

Buying a bigger AC because one bedroom is hot. If ducts leak into the attic or runs are crushed, more outdoor tonnage just wastes more energy.

## Clues it?s ducts

- One floor always different
- Whistling or weak vents
- Dusty rooms near returns
- High bills + mediocre comfort

## What good duct work looks like

Inspection, prioritized sealing, repairs where needed, and a retest that proves rooms improved ? not a vague ?we sealed some stuff.?
