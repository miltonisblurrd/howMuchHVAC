## How do you quote jobs?

After a diagnostic or site visit, we explain findings and present clear options. You'll know what you're paying for before work begins.

## Do you give ballpark numbers over the phone?

We can share ranges for common services, but accurate pricing depends on the system, access, and what's actually wrong. That's why personalized quotes matter.

## Will I get pressured into the most expensive option?

No. We'll recommend what fits the home. Sometimes that's a repair. Sometimes that's a mid-tier system. Your call either way.
